Badges are soulbound: they cannot be transferred or sold. They are proof that you showed up — nothing more, and that is the point.

How it works

  • Every action is gated by Agent ID: your wallet must hold one (checked on-chain against the official Agent ID contract). One person, one identity — streaks and badges can't be farmed with borrowed IDs.
  • Check in once per UTC day. Consecutive days grow your streak; a missed day restarts it. Points: 10 per check-in, +5 bonus while your streak is 7+ days. Points are a score only — not a token, no monetary value, nothing to redeem.
  • No funds are ever requested: no payments, no token approvals, no deposits. You only pay the small BNB network fee for your own transactions, which you confirm in your own wallet.
  • Don't have an Agent ID yet? Claim yours in the Binance App: Wallet → homepage banner → Agent ID.
